Privacy

Privacy, in plain terms

Updated November 8, 2026

What this site collects

We use Google Analytics to see how this website is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city level), and what kind of device and browser they use. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. We look at totals and patterns, not at individual people. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on, and the site will work the same.

If you email us

If you write to us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and do the work. We do not sell email addresses, and we do not share them with anyone for their marketing.

Business outreach

Reaching out to businesses is our work. We contact business representatives using business contact details drawn from public sources and licensed data providers. If you would rather not hear from us, reply to any message and say so, or write to hello@karstagency.com. We will remove you and not contact you again.

Client records

Records created for a client, including meeting records and contact lists, belong to that client.

The short version

We collect as little as we can, we use it only to run this site and this business, and we do not sell personal data. Questions about any of this: hello@karstagency.com.
