Privacy

Privacy, in plain terms

Updated November 24, 2026

What this site collects

We use Google Analytics to see how this website is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city level), and what kind of device and browser they use. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. We look at totals and patterns, not at individual people. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on, and the site will work the same.

If you contact us

If you write to us or book a call, we keep the conversation and the details you share so we can reply, prepare, and do the work. Calls are booked through Calendly, a third-party scheduling service that collects your name and email to set up the appointment. We do not sell email addresses, and we do not share them with anyone for their marketing.

Working with us

Consulting means seeing how a business actually runs. Anything you show us in a session or an engagement — your processes, your numbers, your documents — is treated as your confidential business information. We use it only to do your work, we do not share it, and the records we create for you, including your one-page workflow map, belong to you.

The short version

We collect as little as we can, we use it only to run this site and this business, and we do not sell personal data. Questions about any of this: hello@karstagency.com.
