Before you ask

What do you need from us?

About 30 minutes: a short intake form and read-only access to one CRM. You grant the access, and you can revoke it whenever you like. No meetings, and no credentials ever shared over email.

What if the audit finds nothing badly wrong?

Then your Leak Map says so, system by system, with the evidence to back it up. A clean map is a deliverable, not a failure — and you keep it either way.

Which CRMs do you work in?

Any mainstream one. The audit is about your data and your process, not a specific tool. If your stack is unusual, the first reply will tell you plainly whether we can read it properly.

Do you guarantee more meetings?

No — and you should be wary of anyone who does. We sell systems and standards, measured from your own data. What your team books from a sealed pipeline is theirs to close.

Why is the price flat, and public?

Because the scope is fixed: one CRM, one pipeline, five business days. You should not need a sales process to learn what an audit costs.
