Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

Do you guarantee meetings?

Nobody can honestly guarantee a number, so we guarantee the terms. You pay nothing until meetings are happening, with buyers who fit the profile you approved. If the 60-day pilot doesn't produce three meetings, it costs you nothing, and after the pilot, any month that falls short is free too.

Will this make me look like a spammer?

No. We write short, plain emails to a small number of people who fit, in words you've approved, from separate domains so your main email is never at risk. If someone asks who's writing, the answer is honest: you work with an outside firm on outreach, and you'll be the one on the call.

How much of my time does it take?

One working session up front so we understand who buys from you and why. After that, a few open slots a week, quick replies to the prospects we send your way, and showing up to the meetings.

What if someone doesn't show up?

If the prospect no-shows, it doesn't count. If your side misses a meeting it booked, that one counts.

Why not hire a salesperson, or another agency?

A salesperson is a full salary for months before you know if they can book. Most agencies bill for effort: hours, emails sent, a retainer whether or not anyone shows up. Ours is one flat monthly amount, charged only in months where the meetings land. A month that falls short costs you nothing.

Who is this not for?

Firms where a new customer is not worth enough for the math to work, because the meetings would cost more than the work is worth. Brand-new offers with no customers yet. And anyone who needs customers this month: real outreach takes a few weeks to warm up before the first meetings land.
