Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

What exactly is the working session?

A working session inside your business, not a sales call. We watch how work actually flows: how a job comes in, where it waits, what gets typed twice, what only you can do. We ask a lot of questions and take notes. By the end we can usually point at the one number worth moving first, and what it would take to move it.

What do I walk away with?

A one-page map: the problem in your words, the one number to move, where that number stands today, a 60-day target, and the first build we would recommend. It is yours to keep whether or not we ever work together. Plenty of owners take the map and act on it themselves, and that is fine.

How much does it cost?

The working session is a paid engagement. We quote it plainly on the discovery call, before you commit to anything, and the price does not change afterward. Every step after that is scoped and priced in writing before it starts. No meters, no hourly billing, no surprises.

We are not technical. Is this for us?

Yes. Most of our work is with owners who run good businesses and have no interest in becoming software experts. We handle the technical side. What we need from you is knowledge of your business: where the time goes, where work piles up, and what a good month looks like.

What happens after the session?

Only what you choose. If the map shows a number worth moving, we scope the first build: what it does, what it costs, and how we will know it worked. If the map says AI is not your problem right now, we will tell you that too, and the map is still yours.

Who is this not for?

Brand-new businesses with no customers yet. Anyone shopping for a specific tool before they know which problem it solves. And anyone who needs results this week: honest diagnosis takes a little time, and a build measured against a real number takes a little more.
