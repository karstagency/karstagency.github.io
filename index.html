The problem

Every pipeline has sinkholes.

A form comes in on Tuesday. It gets answered on Friday, by which point the buyer has already booked two competitor calls.

Marketing reports that leads are up. Sales says the leads are junk. Both are reading the same CRM, and neither side can prove its version.

You spent real money to be at that conference, and nobody can say what it produced.

None of these are effort problems — your people are working. They are plumbing problems. And plumbing, unlike effort, can be measured.

Check it yourself

Ten minutes. No access needed.

Fill in your own website's contact form, as a buyer would. Then note three things:

  1. How long until a human replies. An auto-acknowledgement doesn't stop the clock.
  2. Whether the reply answers what you asked, or is a template that could have gone to anyone.
  3. How many follow-ups arrive if you don't respond, and when they stop.

If the first reply took more than an hour, or the follow-ups stopped after one, that is the leak the audit measures properly — across your last 50 leads instead of one. If you'd rather we looked, send us what you found and we'll tell you what it usually means.
