The problem

Referrals arrive when they feel like it.

Most of your work comes from people who already know you. It is good work, and it closes. The problem is the calendar: some months the phone rings, some months it does not, and you cannot plan around weather.

You are also the sales team. Every hour you spend hunting for the next customer is an hour you are not delivering for the current ones, so prospecting is the first thing that slips, every time.

The usual fixes are worse. A full-time salesperson costs thousands a month before a single meeting happens. Ads burn money learning what you already know about your buyers. And the lead sellers want paying whether or not anyone shows up.

What you actually need is simple to say: a steady flow of conversations with the buyers you would pick yourself, that you only pay for when they happen.

How you know

Every meeting comes with its paper trail.

The usual fight in this business is about whether a meeting was real, and whether it was the agency's doing. We designed that fight out. You see the same records we do, as they happen:

  • The send. Every email we send in your name, logged in a workspace you can open.
  • The reply. The prospect's own words, in a shared inbox.
  • The booking record. Who the prospect is, their role and company, and the public source that proves they match your written profile. You see it before the meeting, and you can decline the meeting in advance by naming the part of the profile it fails.
  • The outcome. After each meeting, it is marked held or no-show. A meeting your side does not mark within two business days counts as held. A no-show is never billed, and claiming one takes evidence, not a say-so.
  • The charge. Weekly, itemized, each charge tied to a meeting in the log.

Nobody is on the honor system, in either direction. You cannot be billed for a meeting without a thread, a booking, and a record you saw beforehand. And a meeting that fits the profile you signed counts even if the conversation goes nowhere, because closing it is your end of the work.

Straight answers

Before you ask

Do you guarantee meetings?

We guarantee the terms, and the terms are the guarantee. You pay only for meetings that happen, with buyers who match the profile you approved in writing. If the 60-day pilot does not produce three meetings, you pay nothing for it. What we will not do is promise a count, because a count is the one thing nobody in this business can honestly promise in advance.

What counts as a meeting we pay for?

A meeting that came from our outreach, with a person who matched your written profile when it was booked, who knew the meeting was about your services, and who showed up. If your side books a meeting and does not show, that one counts. If a meeting does not fit the profile, you can decline it before it happens and it never bills.

What do you need from us?

A named person who takes the meetings and lets us send in their name, a written buyer profile, a short list of people we must never contact, about eight open slots a week on that person's calendar, same-day replies to the prospects we route to you, and a card on file. The card is charged only when a meeting happens. Before we start, there is one working session where we look at your recent customers together, because this only works when your offer already sells.

Why not just hire a salesperson?

You can, and for some firms that is right. A full-time hire costs thousands every month in salary alone, months before you know whether they can book, and prospecting is one part of what they end up doing. With us, the cost follows the meetings: nothing in a month where nothing happens.

We tried an agency before. How is this different?

Most agencies bill for effort: hours, emails sent, a retainer whether or not anyone shows up. We bill for one thing, a meeting that happened, and you can check every part of how it came to be in the log. If we do not produce, we do not get paid. That is the whole difference.

Who is this not for?

Firms whose new customer is worth less than about $15,000 in the first year, because the math stops working for you before it stops working for us. Brand-new offers with no customers yet. And anyone who needs customers this month to survive: honest outreach takes a few weeks to warm up before the first meetings land.
